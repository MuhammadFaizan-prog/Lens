## Notes on source reliability

- The FocuSee comparison page (`fs-cmp`) is vendor marketing. Its check-mark grid did not survive text extraction, so only its feature names and prices are used.
- FocuSee states cloud storage as "5 GB" on the pricing page and "at least 1 GB" in the share guide. The inventory reports both.
- Screen Studio's AI upscaler is "6.5× upscale" on the homepage but "upscales up to 4×" in the 4.1 changelog. The inventory reports both.
- The Screen Studio changelog page renders client-side. Its text was read from the page's embedded data payload (4.1, 4.0, 3.0 and earlier notes).
- The FocuSee site moved from focusee.imobie.com to focusee.ai; older URLs redirect.
