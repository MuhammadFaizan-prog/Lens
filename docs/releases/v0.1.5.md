Lens 0.1.5 is the biggest editor release so far: freeze frames and title cards, a music bed that ducks under your voice, platform export presets, an auto-drafted title / description / chapters, several sizes from one recording, voiceover, camera scenes, transitions, an editor hand-off export (alpha overlay + FCPXML / EDL), a cursor that matches what the UI showed, sharper text in zooms and share links to your own storage.

**Upgrading from 0.1.3 or 0.1.4 (you may need one manual install):** if **Update → Restart to update** does not bring you to 0.1.5, download `Lens-Setup-0.1.5.exe` from this page and run it once (quit Lens from the tray first: tray icon → **Quit Lens**). Your settings (`%APPDATA%\Lens`) and recordings (`Videos\Lens`) are kept.

**Windows SmartScreen:** the installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.

## New

- **Freeze frames, still images and title cards** (#20): hold the picture at the playhead, drop in a still image or a full-frame title card (follows your brand kit when it is on).
- **Music with automatic ducking** (#23): a music bed under the whole video (three built-in beds made by Lens, or your own file) that gets quieter while you talk and comes back in the pauses.
- **Platform export presets** (#24): one click sets the canvas size and frame rate for a platform's published upload spec; one undo step.
- **Title, description and chapters from the transcript** (#27): drafted on your device (no AI model, no network), editable, and written into the export. English works best.
- **Several sizes from one recording** (#15): export 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 and 4:5 in one batch, each framed on its own instead of a letterboxed copy.
- **Voiceover** (#17): record narration per transcript line or at the playhead, or import audio files, on a new Voiceover lane.
- **Camera scenes** (#18): switch the webcam layout per part of the video: screen only, camera full frame, side by side or picture-in-picture, each with its own transition.
- **Transitions and enter / exit animations** (#14): Crossfade, Push, Zoom through and Blur dip on cuts (project default or per cut), plus animations for text slides, title cards, the webcam and annotations.
- **Editor hand-off** (#11): export what Lens draws as a transparent overlay (ProRes 4444 or VP9 with alpha) plus an FCPXML and an EDL, so the recording, the cuts and the overlay line up in Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Premiere or After Effects.
- **Cursor that matches the UI** (#12): the drawn cursor shows a hand over links and an I-beam over text fields, as the real screen did. New recordings log the system cursor; recordings without it can get a conservative inferred I-beam (**Match UI hover**); you can override any segment.
- **Sharper text in zooms** (#22): **Zoom → Sharpen zoomed text** keeps small UI text crisp when the camera zooms in.
- **Share link with your own storage** (#13): upload an export to your own S3-compatible bucket and get a link to a small player page. Nothing goes through Lens servers.

## Fixes

- **Export Cancel**: pressing Cancel now marks the export cancelled at once and stops the server job in the background, so a cancel can no longer be lost while the request is in flight.

## Changed behaviour

- **9:16 Follow cursor framing** now letterboxes when the cursor and a zoom target cannot both fit in the frame, instead of cutting one of them off. Projects saved with 9:16 Follow in 0.1.4 may render differently.

## Known limitations

- Tested on **Linux** only; **nothing in this release has been verified on Windows yet**, in particular: the cursor type logging on Windows (GetCursorInfo), the ProRes 4444 / VP9 alpha encoders and importing the FCPXML / EDL into real editors, and the share link's key storage (Windows DPAPI via Electron safeStorage) and uploads to real buckets.
- Music ducks under the microphone only, not under voiceover clips.
- The editor hand-off overlay does not draw cut transitions.
- Users on 0.1.3 or 0.1.4 may need to install 0.1.5 manually once (above).
- The installer is unsigned (SmartScreen, above).
- Logs are at `%APPDATA%\Lens\logs\lens.log`; use **Open log folder** to find them and attach the log when reporting a bug.
