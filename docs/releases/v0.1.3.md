Lens 0.1.3 fixes the Windows bugs reported on 0.1.2, makes recording and opening the editor much faster, and adds caption fonts, canvas layout, timeline multi-select and 3D motion.

**Upgrading:** this is an in-place upgrade from 0.1.2. A running 0.1.2 shows **Update available v0.1.3** (recorder window, tray menu, editor header); click **Update**, then **Restart to update**. Your settings (`%APPDATA%\Lens`) and recordings (`Videos\Lens`) are kept. A fresh download works too.

**Windows SmartScreen:** the installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.

## Windows fixes

- **Custom region**: choosing the Custom card opens the drag overlay straight away, and pressing Record in Custom mode with no area opens it instead of failing with "drag the area to record first". Cancelling leaves the recorder idle.
- **Import Video**: H.264 files that are already in the right shape are copied without re-encoding (a 60 s 1080p file imports in under a second); everything else re-encodes with the same encoder as recordings. The recorder shows **Importing… N%** with a **Cancel** button, and a failure says why.
- **Editor File menu**: the editor now has its own File menu.
- **Faster from Stop to the editor**: finalizing a recording is about 1.5–1.7x faster (encoder probe moved off the Stop path, audio and camera processed alongside the screen, hardware H.264 when it works).
- **Blank editor fixed**: the editor no longer stays black after a recording. It shows a loading screen while it opens and an error banner with the reason if something goes wrong, with **Open log folder** to find the log.
- **Recorder hidden after Stop**: the recorder window steps aside when the editor opens.
- **Webcam**: a recording without a camera shows a clear empty state instead of "no webcam track", **Show webcam** works, the labels fit the inspector at 1280 px, and the camera you picked is remembered.
- **Settings reach the encoder**: Export bitrate and Hardware acceleration in Settings are now actually used by ffmpeg.
- **Inspector scrolls**, so no control is cut off on smaller windows.
- **Editor opens fast**: the editor no longer probes the videos synchronously on open; the first frame shows in about 2 s instead of over a minute for a 60 s 1080p60 recording with camera.

## New

- **Caption fonts and styles**: 12 caption fonts (bundled, with their licences) and 6 caption styles.
- **Canvas layout**: pick an aspect ratio that fits your output, then move, resize and crop the screen card on the canvas.
- **Timeline multi-select**: select several items (click, marquee), drag them together, delete them at once.
- **3D motion**: a 3D motion lane with 13 templates, custom angles, keyframes, and **Save as template** for your own; rendered the same in preview and export.

## Performance (v0.1.2 vs v0.1.3)

| Case | v0.1.2 | v0.1.3 | Speed-up |
|---|---|---|---|
| Finalize 10 s screen + mic | 9.84 s | 5.72 s | 1.7x |
| Finalize 10 s + camera | 11.40 s | 7.07 s | 1.6x |
| Finalize 60 s screen + mic | 56.16 s | 34.93 s | 1.6x |
| Finalize 60 s + camera | 65.69 s | 43.49 s | 1.5x |
| Import 60 s 1080p30 H.264 mp4 | 33.29 s | 0.35 s | ~95x |
| Import 60 s 1080p30 VP9 webm | 43.58 s | 24.80 s | 1.8x |
| Editor open: bundle ready (60 s 1080p60 + camera) | 79.99 s | 0.28 s | ~290x |
| Editor open: first frame on screen | 80.04 s | 2.30 s | ~35x |

Measured on a 2-core arm64 Linux sandbox with no GPU, so every encode used software libx264 (median of 2 runs, about ±15 % noise). A Windows PC with a hardware H.264 encoder should finalize faster; not measured. New recordings and re-encoded imports are somewhat larger files than in 0.1.2 (faster encoder preset at the same visual target).

## Known limitations

- Tested on the packaged Linux build; **not yet tested on Windows**.
- Recordings made before 0.1.2 still have the Windows cursor baked into the video (#9, partly fixed). If you see two cursors in an older recording, turn off **Lens cursor** in the editor.
- Logs are at `userData/logs/lens.log` (on Windows `%APPDATA%\Lens\logs\lens.log`); use **Open log folder** to find them and attach the log when reporting a bug.
- Caption translation is still disabled in the desktop app.
- The installer is unsigned (SmartScreen, above).
